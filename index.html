1 Your First Separation in 60 Seconds

UVR5 can look intimidating at first because it bundles multiple AI engines in one window. In practice, you only ever configure three steps before clicking Start:

Step 1 · Files & Format

Choose Input & Output

Click Select Input to pick one or more songs. Click Select Output to pick a destination folder, then select WAV (best for DAWs).

Step 2 · Engine & Model

Pick Mode & Stems

Under Choose Process Method, pick MDX-Net (vocals/inst) or Demucs (multi-stem instruments), select a model, and check the stems you want.

Step 3 · Hardware & Run

Set GPU/CPU & Start

Check GPU Conversion only if you have an NVIDIA CUDA GPU (leave unchecked for CPU/Mac/Intel graphics), then click Start Processing.

Pro Tip: Use "Sample Mode" Before Waiting on a Full Song!

Testing different AI models on a full 4-minute track (especially on a CPU) can take several minutes per attempt. Check Sample Mode (you can adjust its length between 5–30 seconds inside the Wrench Settings) to process just a short preview snippet in seconds. Once the snippet sounds clean, uncheck Sample Mode and run the full song.

Right-Column Settings Explained (GPU vs. CPU Guidance)

Setting Name Applies To Recommended Value What It Actually Does
GPU Conversion All Modes ON (NVIDIA CUDA) / OFF (CPU & Integrated GPU) Uses an NVIDIA CUDA graphics card for 5–20× faster processing. If you have Intel/AMD integrated graphics or run on CPU, keep this unchecked to prevent crashes.
WAV / FLAC / MP3 Top Bar WAV (for DAWs) Always export uncompressed WAV when importing stems into Ableton Live, FL Studio, or Logic Pro to avoid compression artifacts.
Window Size VR Arch 512 (balanced) or 320 (fast) Controls analysis resolution. 1024 can sound slightly smoother on reverb removal but uses more memory and runs slower; 512 is the sweet spot.
Aggression Setting VR Arch 5 (default) Range 0–50. Raise to 7–10 if vocal bleed still leaks into the instrumental; lower to 2–4 if extracted vocals sound thin or phasey.
Segment Size MDX-Net / Demucs 256 or Default (CPU / 4GB VRAM) · 512 (8GB+ VRAM) Chunks audio for neural-net processing. Smaller chunks (128–256) save RAM/VRAM and prevent out-of-memory crashes while keeping quality intact.
Overlap MDX-Net / Demucs 0.25 (default) Blends adjacent audio chunks smoothly. Higher values (e.g., 0.5 or 0.75) can smooth tiny seams at the cost of significantly slower processing.
Shifts Demucs 0–1 (CPU) · 2 (GPU) Runs the AI multiple times with tiny time offsets and averages them. Shifts = 2 doubles processing time; keep at 0 or 1 on CPU.

3 The 4 Core Separation Modes — What Each One Does Best

Different AI architectures specialize in different jobs. UVR v5.6 ships with starter models pre-installed, and unlocks dozens more specialized models for free inside the built-in Download Center.

1. MDX-Net

Default + Upgrades

Best for: Surgical 2-stem separation (Vocals vs. Instrumental) and ultra-clean backing tracks.

  • Included in v5.6: UVR-MDX-NET-Inst_HQ_3 — one of the best all-around models for clean instrumentals and crisp vocal extracts.
  • Top Download Center Picks:
    • Kim_Vocal_2 — renowned for clean, natural lead vocals.
    • MDX23C-8KFFT-InstVoc_HQ — studio-grade full-bandwidth vocal/instrumental separation.
    • UVR-MDX-NET-Karaoke-2 — strips lead vocal while keeping backing harmonies inside the instrumental.

2. Demucs (v4)

Multi-Stem Engine

Best for: Splitting a full mix into individual instrument stems (Drums, Bass, Guitar, Piano, Vocals, Other).

  • Included in v5.6: v4 | htdemucs — splits any song into 4 stems: Vocals, Drums, Bass, and Other (synths/guitars/keys).
  • Stem Options: Check All Stems to export all 4 tracks at once, or pick one target stem (e.g., Drums) to isolate it plus everything else.
  • Top Download Center Picks:
    • v4 | htdemucs_6s — unlocks 6 stems (adds dedicated Guitar and Piano tracks!).
    • v4 | htdemucs_ft — fine-tuned 4-stem version (higher quality, ~4× slower).

3. VR Architecture

Cleanup & FX Removal

Best for: Audio restoration & cleanup—removing reverb, echo/delay, background noise, and separating harmonies.

  • Included in v5.6:
    • 1_HP-UVR — general vocal/instrumental high-precision model.
    • UVR-DeNoise-Lite — cleans hiss, hum, and static from recordings or extracted stems.
  • Essential Download Center Picks:
    • UVR-De-Echo-Normal / Aggressive & UVR-DeEcho-DeReverb — strips baked-in room reverb and delay tails from vocals so you can apply your own DAW reverb!
    • 5_HP-Karaoke-UVR / 6_HP-Karaoke-UVR — splits lead vocals from backing harmonies.

4. Ensemble Mode

Advanced Blending

Best for: Combining 2 or more models (for example, MDX-Net + Demucs) so each model hides the other's artifacts.

  • How It Works: UVR5 runs each selected model sequentially and mathematically blends their frequency spectra.
  • Key Algorithms:
    • Max Spec / Min Spec — selects the stronger or cleaner spectral estimate per stem (great for removing faint instrumental bleed from vocals).
    • Average — balances two vocal models smoothly.
  • Save Time: Use the Ensemble preset controls to save your favorite 2-model combination for one-click reuse.

4 How to Download New Models (The Download Center)

UVR v5.6 installs with 4 core starter models, while specialized models (such as 6-stem Demucs, De-Reverb, and Kim_Vocal_2) can be downloaded in seconds right inside the app:

  1. Click the Wrench Icon in the bottom-left area of UVR5 to open the Settings window.
  2. Switch to the Download Center tab at the top of the Settings window.
  3. Select a category radio button: VR Arch, MDX-Net, or Demucs.
  4. Open the dropdown menu underneath, choose the model you want (for example, Demucs v4: htdemucs_6s or VR Arch: UVR-DeEcho-DeReverb), and click the Download Arrow icon next to it.
  5. Wait for the download bar to finish, close Settings, and the new model will appear immediately in your main window's Choose Model dropdown.

5 Step-by-Step Recipes for Music Producers & DAWs

Recipe 1: Studio-Clean "Dry" Lead Vocals (For Remixes & Sampling) 2-Pass Workflow
  1. Pass 1 (Isolate the Vocals): Set Process Method to MDX-Net → Model UVR-MDX-NET-Inst_HQ_3 (or downloaded Kim_Vocal_2) → check Vocals Only → Format WAV → run Start Processing.
  2. Pass 2 (Optional — Strip Reverb & Backing Harmonies): Drag your newly created vocal .wav back into Select Input. Switch Process Method to VR Architecture → run UVR-DeEcho-DeReverb to dry out the vocal, or 5_HP-Karaoke-UVR to isolate the lead line from harmonies.
  3. Into Your DAW: Drag the dry .wav onto an Audio Track (e.g., in Ableton Live, enable Warp with Complex Pro mode) and add your own EQ, compression, and reverb cleanly.
Recipe 2: Clean Instrumental / Karaoke Backing Track Starter Model Ready
  1. Select your song in Select Input and choose an output folder.
  2. Set Process Method to MDX-Net → select UVR-MDX-NET-Inst_HQ_3 (or UVR-MDX-NET-Karaoke-2 if you want to keep backing harmonies).
  3. Check Instrumental Only (or leave both checkboxes unchecked if you want both Vocals and Instrumental saved).
  4. Set format to WAV (or MP3 for lightweight practice tracks) and click Start Processing.
Recipe 3: Full Multi-Track Stems (Drums, Bass, Vocals, Instruments) 4 or 6 Stems
  1. Set Process Method to Demucs → choose v4 | htdemucs (4 stems) or download v4 | htdemucs_6s (6 stems with Guitar & Piano).
  2. Check All Stems in the center column and set Format to WAV.
  3. Set Shifts to 0–1 on CPU (or 2 on NVIDIA GPU) and click Start Processing.
  4. Drag all 4 (or 6) exported .wav files simultaneously into your DAW (in Ableton Live, hold Ctrl while dropping into Arrangement View so every stem lands on its own time-aligned track!).
Recipe 4: Using Built-In "Audio Tools" (Pitch, Tempo & Mastering Match) Bonus Utilities
  • Time Stretch: Speed up or slow down any stem without changing pitch using UVR5's built-in Rubberband engine.
  • Change Pitch: Shift an instrumental or acapella up/down by semitones before importing into your project.
  • Matchering: Automatically match the loudness and tonal EQ balance of your track to a commercial reference track.

Speed & Troubleshooting Checklist
  • Processing seems frozen? Without an NVIDIA GPU, CPU processing for a full 3–4 minute track in Demucs or Ensemble mode can take 3–10+ minutes. Watch the log window at the bottom or test with Sample Mode first.
  • Out-of-memory / CUDA error? Lower Segment Size in MDX-Net/Demucs (e.g., from 512 down to 256 or 128), close heavy DAW projects while UVR5 runs, or uncheck GPU Conversion if your GPU lacks VRAM.
  • Vocals sound metallic or "underwater"? Try a gentler model (Kim_Vocal_2), lower VR Aggression to 2–4, or compare Demucs vs. MDX-Net in Sample Mode.
